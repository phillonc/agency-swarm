## SECTION 1: MARKETING & SOCIAL MEDIA (Prompts 1–20)

**1. Weekly Social Media Content Calendar**
> Create a 7-day social media content calendar for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. Include post ideas for Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. My target audience is [AUDIENCE]. My brand voice is [PROFESSIONAL/CASUAL/FUN]. Include suggested posting times.

**2. Instagram Caption Generator**
> Write 5 engaging Instagram captions for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] promoting [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include relevant hashtags, a call to action, and use a [TONE] voice. Keep each under 150 words.

**3. Facebook Ad Copy**
> Write 3 versions of Facebook ad copy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Highlight these benefits: [BENEFITS]. Include a strong headline, body text, and call-to-action button text.

**4. Google Business Profile Description**
> Write an SEO-optimized Google Business Profile description for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] located in [CITY]. We specialise in [SERVICES]. Include relevant keywords and a compelling reason to choose us over competitors.

**5. Email Newsletter Subject Lines**
> Generate 10 email subject lines for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] newsletter about [TOPIC]. Optimise for open rates. Include a mix of curiosity-driven, benefit-driven, and urgency-driven subject lines.

**6. Blog Post Outline**
> Create a detailed blog post outline for the topic "[TOPIC]" for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] website. Include an engaging introduction, 5-7 subheadings with bullet points, and a conclusion with a call to action. Optimise for the keyword "[KEYWORD]."

**7. Competitor Analysis Prompt**
> Analyse the marketing strategies a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] could use to compete with [COMPETITOR NAME/TYPE]. Suggest 5 ways I can differentiate my brand and win more customers.

**8. Customer Testimonial Request**
> Write a friendly email asking a satisfied customer of my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] to leave a review on [PLATFORM]. Make it easy for them by including a direct link placeholder and suggested talking points.

**9. Viral TikTok/Reels Ideas**
> Give me 10 short-form video ideas for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] on TikTok or Instagram Reels. Each should be under 60 seconds, easy to film with a phone, and designed to get shares.

**10. Seasonal Promotion Campaign**
> Plan a [HOLIDAY/SEASON] marketing campaign for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. Include email copy, social media posts, a promotional offer structure, and a timeline for execution.

**11. Brand Story Template**
> Help me write a compelling brand story for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. I started because [REASON]. My mission is [MISSION]. My customers love us because [REASON].

**12. Pinterest Pin Descriptions**
> Write 5 SEO-optimised Pinterest pin descriptions for [PRODUCT/SERVICE/BLOG POST]. Include relevant keywords and a call to action.

**13. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post**
> Write a LinkedIn post where I share a lesson I learned about [TOPIC] as a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] owner. Make it conversational, include a hook in the first line, and end with a question to drive engagement.

**14. Referral Programme Launch**
> Design a referral programme for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. Include the incentive structure, email announcement copy, social media posts, and terms and conditions outline.

**15. Local SEO Content Ideas**
> Give me 10 blog post or page ideas that will help my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in [CITY] rank higher in local Google searches.

**16. Press Release Template**
> Write a press release for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] announcing [NEWS — e.g., grand opening, new product, milestone]. Include a headline, subheadline, quotes, and boilerplate.

**17. Hashtag Strategy**
> Create a hashtag strategy for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] on Instagram. Give me 30 hashtags divided into: 10 broad/popular, 10 niche-specific, and 10 local/community hashtags.

**18. YouTube Video Script**
> Write a 5-minute YouTube script for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] video about [TOPIC]. Include a hook, introduction, main content with 3 key points, and a call to action to subscribe.

**19. Re-engagement Email Sequence**
> Write a 3-email re-engagement sequence for inactive customers of my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. Goal: get them to [DESIRED ACTION]. Space them 3 days apart.

**20. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**
> Help me define a clear USP for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. We offer [SERVICES/PRODUCTS], our target market is [AUDIENCE], and our competitors are [COMPETITORS]. What makes us different?
